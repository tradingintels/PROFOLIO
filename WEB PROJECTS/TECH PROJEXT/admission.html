Admissions

Start your tech career with Mobiq

Explore our admission requirements, process, and the easy path to enrolling in a course designed for real-world success.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed Admission Application Form
  • Two Recent Passport Photographs
  • Valid Means of Identification (National ID, Voter's Card, International Passport, or Driver's Licence)
  • Copies of Academic Certificates or Statements of Result (where applicable)
  • Birth Certificate or Declaration of Age (if required)
  • Proof of Payment of the Application Fee (if applicable)

Note: Some short certificate courses may not require previous academic qualifications.

Why Choose Mobiq Tech Academy?

  • Industry-focused and practical technology training
  • Experienced and certified instructors
  • Modern computer laboratories with up-to-date equipment
  • High-speed internet access for learning and research
  • Hands-on projects and real-world practical experience

  • Career guidance and mentorship
  • Internship and job placement support
  • Flexible weekday and weekend schedules
  • Affordable tuition with flexible payment options
  • Supportive environment encouraging innovation

Admission Process

  1. Complete the online application form
  2. Submit required documents
  3. Application review by admissions team
  4. Receive admission offer
  5. Pay tuition fees
  6. Registration and orientation
  7. Begin your studies
