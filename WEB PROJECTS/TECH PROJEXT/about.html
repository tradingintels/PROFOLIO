About Us

Mobiq Tech Academy (MTA) is a technology education and digital innovation institution committed to developing globally competitive digital professionals. As a member of the Mobiq ecosystem, the Academy empowers students, professionals, entrepreneurs, and organizations with practical, industry-relevant technology skills through hands-on training, mentorship, and professional certification.

Our mission is to bridge the digital skills gap by providing quality technology education that prepares learners for employment, entrepreneurship, innovation, and leadership in the digital economy.

Vision & Mission

🎯 Vision

To become Africa's leading technology academy, producing globally competitive digital professionals, innovators, and entrepreneurs.

🚀 Mission

To deliver world-class technology education through practical training, professional certifications, innovation, mentorship, and career development.
