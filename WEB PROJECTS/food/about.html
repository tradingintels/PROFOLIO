WHO WE ARE

A Modern African Agribusiness Built for the Future

Moobiq Food is the food production, processing, distribution, and agribusiness division of the Moobiq ecosystem. We are dedicated to delivering safe, nutritious, affordable, and premium-quality food products while supporting sustainable agriculture, food security, and economic empowerment throughout Africa.

Our operations span the entire agricultural value chain—from commercial farming and food processing to packaging, logistics, retail, wholesale distribution, exports, and agricultural support services. We collaborate with farmers, cooperatives, businesses, governments, and development partners to create lasting impact.

Mission & Vision

Driven by innovation, sustainability, and excellence, our purpose is to feed communities while creating opportunities throughout Africa.

To produce, process, and distribute nutritious food products through innovation, sustainable farming practices, strategic partnerships, and customer-focused services that improve lives, strengthen communities, and contribute to Africa's economic development.
